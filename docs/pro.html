"Your idea deserves a try. Let's make something and see what happens."

I built Vo-Coder the way I made music: without first checking how everyone else does it. No research first, no copying the market. Just the idea, built until it works, and only afterwards a look around to see what others did.

Sometimes I reinvent the wheel. More often I end up somewhere nobody else went: a coding harness that grew into a 3D world, a music studio with a mixing console you can sit at, a live visual stage, a smart-home twin and a team of AI agents you can watch flying to the files they're editing.

It started as the tool shed: interchangeable models picking up tools and getting to work. Its roots are in Vodomation OS, a custom Linux desktop where applications grow with you. Vo-Coder was rebuilt from scratch to bring that idea to Windows, macOS and Linux. I use it every day, and every workflow, every screen and every piece of logic in it comes from that use.

01World view

Your project is a place you can walk into.

Every project starts as an empty world. As you and your agents create folders and files, a city grows. Folders become platforms, files become buildings, and code becomes walls you can read up close.

  • Agents fly to their work. When an agent edits a file, it travels to that building. Follow it and see exactly where it's working.
  • Read the walls. Click a code wall, scroll it, select a line and ask about what you're looking at. No need to explain where it is.
  • Explore on foot. Walk with WASD, run, jump between rooftops, take off and land. Or fly freely, follow an agent, or pull back to see the whole city.
  • Studios have their own platforms. Audio, video, image, label, web and 3D work happen at stations with real equipment and live screens.
  • Atmosphere you control. Drifting mist, galaxies, world music that keeps playing across views, and a 30 / 48 / 60 fps setting.
  • Off when you want it off. Switch the world off and the 3D scene unloads completely. The normal workspace works the same either way.

03Your AI team

Any model. Your agents. One coordinator.

Give agents names, roles, instructions, tools and a model. Mix cloud providers, local models and the coding CLIs you already pay for. Vodo coordinates them, hands out work and checks what comes back.

  • Cloud: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, xAI, Z.ai, NVIDIA.
  • Local: Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp and FLM on an NPU, with named endpoints across your machines.
  • Light on small models: a local model starts with only a few tools and a list of tool categories. It opens one category, such as audio or web, without reading the rest, so its context window stays free for the work.
  • Tool scripts: a model can run several tools in one step by writing a short script, and gets back one answer. The script runs in a sealed sandbox with no access to files or the network, and every tool it calls asks for approval as usual.
  • CLIs: Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Kimi, Muse and Antigravity run with their own login, so there's no key to store.
  • Routing: let Vodo pick a model by task difficulty and cost, prefer your own agents, or switch routing off.
  • Colleagues or hired help: give an agent project memory to make it a colleague, or leave it out for a focused worker that does only its brief.

04Design suite

Seven studios. The same agent in every one.

Video, audio, 3D, live visuals, images, packaging labels and web pages. Each studio has its own tools, and Vodo works inside it with you, in the same conversation, on the same model.

Audio: a real DAW, with a room to sit in

Arrange, MIDI editing, a full mixer, an effects rack and a patchbay, running on a native low-latency audio engine. It hosts VST3 and CLAP plugins over WASAPI or ASIO. Switch to the 3D view and you're sitting at a console in a studio room, moving the real faders.

What the audio studio doesAudio
  • Ask for music: "a four-bar drum beat at 95 BPM", then "a bass line under it". The notes land in the song.
  • Plugin Lab: describe a sound ("a gritty tape delay with a dark tone") and build your own effect or instrument.
  • Oscilloscope drawing: draw shapes in stereo audio that show up on an XY scope.
  • Built-in instruments, audio tracks, buses, groups, automation and elastic timing.
  • Export to WAV, MP3 or FLAC, or generate takes with a music model you've configured.

3D Studio + Blender

A scene you share with Vodo. Compose shapes, import GLB, FBX, OBJ and more, pose parts and bones, record keyframes and export animated GLB. When the job needs more, Vodo writes Blender Python, runs it, and the result comes back into the studio.

Label & packaging

Packaging labels with layers, barcodes and templates, a 3D mockup and a print-ready dieline, with Design AI that edits the artwork for you.

VisualizerLive

An empty live stage. Ask for a mountain, a growing city or a flight through clouds, then shape it by voice while it plays. Layers have sliders, scenes can be saved, and output goes to a second display. Built for VJ sets.

VideoTimeline

Edit, grade and mix footage on a timeline with ripple, roll, slip and razor tools, keyframe lanes, color, compositing, transitions and markers. Export MP4 or MOV.

Web pageLive edit

Edit a real website, page by page, right on the page: text, images, fonts and hyperlinks, with Design AI at your side. Agents can add pages, add, change and remove elements and place images too, sharing your undo history.

ImageCanvas

Layered artwork on a free canvas for posters, social posts and graphics, with your configured image generator.

Engine bridgesUnity · Unreal

Connect Unity or Unreal projects through managed MCP bridges so agents can use the editor's own tools.

Generated mediaImages · Video

Image and video generation from the chat, saved to the project and shown in the workspace. A vision model describes images for agents that can't see.

05The soul and the brain

Your assistant's identity, your rules, decisions, tasks and history live outside any one model. Swap a cloud model for a local one and the project still remembers what it's in the middle of. Vodo sleeps and dreams: conversations are archived, then distilled into a map of what matters.

Lessons
